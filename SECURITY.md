# Security Policy

We take the security of NaLaMap seriously and appreciate responsible disclosure.

## Supported versions

Only the latest `main` branch and what is currently deployed from it receive security fixes. Older commits, forks and unmaintained branches are not supported.

## Scope

In scope: the code in this repository (backend, frontend and container configuration) and the official container images built from it. Self-hosted deployments are operated by their owners; we can only fix issues in the code we publish.

Out of scope: social engineering or phishing of staff, physical attacks, denial-of-service or load testing, spam, and findings that need a compromised device or account of the victim. Reports about third-party services should go to the respective vendor.

## How to report

Email **security@nalamap.org**. Please do **not** open a public issue, pull request or discussion for a suspected vulnerability.

Include, as far as you can:

- affected component, URL or file, and version or commit
- a description of the issue and its potential impact
- clear steps to reproduce, or a proof of concept
- any logs, screenshots or requests that help us verify it
- how you would like to be credited, if at all

Do not include real personal data, secrets or other people's data beyond the minimum needed to demonstrate the issue. If you encounter any, stop and tell us.

## What to expect

- **Acknowledgement** within 3 business days.
- **Triage** with an initial assessment (severity, affected area) within 10 business days.
- **Updates** on fix progress at least every 30 days until resolution.
- **Coordinated disclosure:** we aim to fix confirmed issues within 90 days of the report and ask you to keep details private until then, or until we agree on a date. We will credit you in the advisory if you wish.

## Safe harbour

We will not pursue legal action against, or ask law enforcement to investigate, researchers who act in good faith under this policy: they make a reasonable effort to avoid privacy violations, data destruction and service disruption, only access what is needed to demonstrate the issue, do not exfiltrate or retain data, report promptly, and do not disclose before we have had a chance to fix. This is not a licence to test systems of third parties, and it does not cover activity that breaks the law outside this policy.

## Bounty

We do not currently operate a bug bounty programme and cannot promise a reward, but we are happy to acknowledge valuable reports.
